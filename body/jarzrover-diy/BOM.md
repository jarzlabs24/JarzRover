# JarzRover bill of materials

The reference build uses:

- 3D-printed body, top plate, phone mount, and wheels
- Four TT gear motors and tires
- Arduino Nano compatible with ATmega328P firmware
- L298N motor driver
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor
- Optional front wheel-speed sensors
- Battery pack, switch, and suitable connectors
- USB cable and phone-compatible USB OTG adapter
- Dupont wires, insulation, and strain relief hardware

Confirm voltage, current, connector, and mechanical compatibility for the parts you choose.
